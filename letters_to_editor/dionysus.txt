To the Architect of the Dionysus Program,

You have named a system of schedules and covenants after the god of dissolution. The contradiction deserves attention.

Apollo has supplied much of the method: rules, roles, tests, and an appointed time to stop.


You are correct about the vacuum. When your stories break, when your explanations fail, the cold wind comes in. You are right that men will burn anything to keep warm—usually each other. Your anti-scapegoat is a clever substitution. It is a very civilized magic to teach a tribe to murder a document instead of a person.

But a disagreement may not end when the meeting ends. A role put in question may not recover its authority because a steward declares Run Time. The consequences extend beyond the calendar.

If trust is weak, rules may not hold. If trust is strong, the group may still arrive at a conclusion the convener did not intend. You must allow for both.

A protected process can make change possible. It cannot promise you control of the result.


Not Dionysus
